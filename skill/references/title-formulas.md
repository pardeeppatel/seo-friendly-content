# SEO Title Formulas, Power Words & Sentiment Guide

> **Developed by Pardeep Kumar** | **Powered by [ToolNPlay.com](https://ToolNPlay.com)**

Use this guide in **Phase 2** to craft 10 distinct, high-CTR SEO titles that satisfy on-page SEO and title readability checks without resorting to clickbait.

---

## 1. Non-Negotiable Title Constraints
1. **Length:** **`≤ 60` characters** total (desktop SERPs display ~60 chars; mobile ~50 chars).
2. **Keyword Position:** Primary Focus Keyword appears within the **first 50%** (ideally within the **first 50 characters**).
3. **Number:** Include a relevant number (`5`, `7`, `10`, `2026`, `30-Day`, etc.) when natural for the topic.
4. **Power Word:** Include at least one proven power word.
5. **Sentiment (No Clickbait):** Convey clear curiosity, urgency, confidence, or problem-solving relief—never exaggerate beyond what the article actually delivers (prevents pogo-sticking).

---

## 2. Curated Power Words (Non-Spammy, High-Trust)

* **Action & Speed:** `Actionable`, `Fast`, `Instant`, `Quick`, `Simple`, `Step-by-Step`, `Practical`, `Easy`
* **Authority & Proof:** `Proven`, `Verified`, `Data-Backed`, `Tested`, `Complete`, `Definitive`, `Real`, `Honest`
* **Value & Advantage:** `Essential`, `Best`, `Smart`, `Effective`, `High-Converting`, `Profitable`, `Free`, `Reliable`
* **Curiosity & Avoidance:** `Hidden`, `Overlooked`, `Costly`, `Critical`, `Mistakes`, `Avoid`, `Fixes`, `Checklist`

---

## 3. Sentiment Triggers (Positive & Negative)
* **Positive (Confidence / Gain):** `Effortless`, `Smart`, `Reliable`, `Clear`, `Winning`, `Boost`, `Master`, `Save`
* **Negative (Risk / Loss Aversion — high CTR for troubleshooting):** `Costly`, `Broken`, `Failing`, `Slow`, `Common`, `Risky`, `Stop`, `Fix`, `Mistakes`

---

## 4. Intent-Based Title Templates (Mix Across the 10 Suggestions)

Vary the angle across the 10 options instead of repeating one formula:

1. **How-To / Process:** `[Focus Keyword]: [Number] Proven Steps to [Outcome]`
2. **Listicle / Tactics:** `[Number] Practical [Focus Keyword] Tips That Actually Work`
3. **Mistakes / Fixes:** `[Number] Costly [Focus Keyword] Mistakes (And Fast Fixes)`
4. **Beginner / Complete Guide:** `[Focus Keyword] Guide: [Number] Essential Rules for [Year]`
5. **Comparison / Best Of:** `[Number] Best [Focus Keyword] Options Tested for [Audience]`
6. **Checklist / Framework:** `[Focus Keyword] Checklist: [Number] Steps for Better Results`
7. **Speed / Outcome:** `[Focus Keyword] Made Simple: [Number] Quick Wins`
8. **Data / Case Study:** `[Focus Keyword] in [Year]: [Number] Data-Backed Lessons`
9. **Contrarian / Reality Check:** `Why Most [Focus Keyword] Fails ([Number] Honest Fixes)`
10. **Template / Playbook:** `[Focus Keyword] Playbook: [Number] Tested Templates`

> **Tip:** Always count exact characters for every suggested title and adjust wording so the Focus Keyword stays in the first half and total length stays `≤ 60` characters.
